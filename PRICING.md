# Pricing

Internal notes, not published on the site. Written 2026-10-30, revised the same day after
a search for 2026 market data (see [Market data](#market-data-october-2026)).

Profile: 25+ years, 7 years as CTO, EU institutions and Luxembourg finance/aviation clients,
Azure/AWS certifications, uncommon mix of sovereign cloud, DDD, agentic AI and facilitation.

## What the data says, in short

- Most published figures describe **staffing missions sold through ESNs and recruiters**.
  Those sit far below the first estimates: Luxembourg listings have a median of €525, and
  architects get about €800–1,000.
- **Direct advisory and leadership work** (fractional CTO, specialist assessments, workshops)
  is priced on a different scale: about €1,000–1,500/day in France and Benelux.
- So there are two tracks with two price lists. Don't let the staffing track set the advisory rate.

**Anchor:** standard direct rate **€1,050/day**. Floor **€900/day** for direct work and
about **€800/day** for long full-time missions through an intermediary.

## Public sector and EU institutions

EU framework contracts usually go through a prime contractor, and the tender sets the rates.
The Luxembourg public-sector listing median is €550, a rate offered to the freelancer.
Expect roughly €650–900/day for an architect-level profile. Worth taking for stability and
references, but it does not set the private-client rate.

## Checking the numbers

- Plan on 140–170 billable days a year, not 220 (sales, writing, #play14, holidays).
- €1,050 × 155 days ≈ €163k billed; a year mostly through intermediaries at €850 ≈ €132k.
  Both before social contributions, tax, insurance, equipment and slow months.
- Work out the minimum needed per year first, then check the floor rate still covers it.

## Rules

- No prices on the site: "ask for a quote" fits the positioning and every lead goes through the form.
- Discount for commitment (5–10% for 6+ months), never because a client asks.
- Half-day units, 2-day minimum for advisory work; cancellation terms for workshops.
- Don't underprice the first clients after leaving Agile Partner: first rates become references.
- Intermediaries keep an estimated 10–20% of the client rate. When a client asks for a rate,
  quote the direct rate, not the one an ESN offered.

## Market data (October 2026)

Search run on 2026-10-30. Most sources are vendors, recruiters or platforms, so treat them as
directional. No source gives a Luxembourg CTO day rate.

**Luxembourg**
- freelancers.lu, 139 listings with a rate (Sept 2025 – Sept 2026), mostly from ESNs:
  median €525 (quartiles €505 / €628). Data & AI €650, cybersecurity €615, PM & agile €610,
  DevOps/cloud €535. Public sector €550, banking €540.
- freelancers.lu, 42 freelancer profiles: median desired rate €665, €700 beyond 8 years.
- freelancers.lu ranges (June 2026, no method given): architect (solution, enterprise, cloud)
  €800–1,000 "sometimes more", senior dev/lead €650–800, cybersecurity €700–900.
- Expatica: senior consultants often above €900/day.

**France**
- TJMètre (14,202 observations): cloud architect median €750 (P25 €640, P75 €820);
  "Directeur de projet / CTO" median €850.
- Hays 5–8 years: cloud architect €700, IT project manager €730.
- Freelance Radar: architect median €600 (€513–750). Consulting grids: senior cloud architect
  €850–1,100.

**Belgium**
- dailyrate.be (905 submissions, March 2026, all roles): median €700, average €710.
- Freelancing in Belgium 2025 report: average about €708/day; IT and business consultants are
  the most likely to bill above €1,000.
- espero-soft guide: senior cloud architect €700–900, senior enterprise architect €800–1,000.

**Germany** (for reference): freelancermap 2026, 5,412 independents, IT median €95/hour
(≈ €720–960/day).

**Fractional CTO, Europe**
- Highcircl: France €1,000–1,500, Netherlands €1,100–1,600, Germany €1,200–1,800.
- 941 Consulting: €700–1,800 across UK, France, Germany, Benelux. First-time CTOs €600–900;
  serial CTOs with board experience €1,700–2,800+.
- Retainers: €6–12k/month for 1–2 days a week.
